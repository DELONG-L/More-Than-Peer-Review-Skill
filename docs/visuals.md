# Visual assets and sources / 视觉资源与来源

[English home](../README.md) · [中文首页](../README.zh-CN.md)

The covers use warm ivory paper, charcoal text, restrained burgundy margin marks, and pale rose highlights. Regular book-serif typography in English and Songti in Chinese keep the layout close to academic reading and editorial annotation.

封面使用暖纸白、炭灰文字、克制的酒红批注与浅玫瑰色标记。英文采用常规书籍衬线字形，中文采用宋体，呼应论文细读与编辑批注。

The annotated paper and feedback sheet are abstract illustrations of the claim–mechanism–evidence workflow. Generic line rules stand in for document text. They are not real manuscripts, review drafts, or application screenshots, and contain no research results or reviewer identities.

带批注的纸张与意见页以抽象形式说明主张、机制与证据之间的评审关系，文档内容仅用通用线条表示。它们不是真实稿件、评审草稿或应用截图，不包含研究结果与审稿人身份。

The content follows the public [skill instructions](../more-than-peer-review/SKILL.md), [conceptual review guidance](../more-than-peer-review/references/conceptual-and-systems-review.md), and [submission review guidance](../more-than-peer-review/references/submission-review-guidelines.md). No private review material or external photographs are used.

内容依据公开的[技能说明](../more-than-peer-review/SKILL.md)、[概念与系统评审指导](../more-than-peer-review/references/conceptual-and-systems-review.md)及[评审意见指导](../more-than-peer-review/references/submission-review-guidelines.md)，未使用私人评审材料或外部摄影作品。
